Sign up (Libera.Chat and other networks that use NickServ)

1) Fill in the nick and the password you want above and press Save. 2) Enter your email here and press Sign up: the app connects as a guest and sends REGISTER to NickServ for you. 3) The network emails you a verification code: paste it below. 4) Press Save & connect again with “log in with SASL” ticked. Your email is used for that one command and is not stored here.

Other networks may word things differently: the replies appear in the status box above, and you can also type commands such as /ns help in the chat window.
