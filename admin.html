Event look

Switch a preset on for everyone for a while (Halloween, winter holidays, a party). People can opt out in their settings. It switches itself off when the time is up.

These are the local chat accounts. Each one has its own IRC login, which you can see here (server and nick) but never its password. Disabling an account hangs up its IRC connection; deleting it also deletes its saved login and chat logs. IRC rules (who can speak where) are set by the IRC network, not here.

Presets

The one-click looks shown at sign-up and in settings. Saving here replaces presets.json for the whole room (the file is kept and “Reset” brings it back).

Database copies

    Copies contain password hashes, your members' IRC logs and their saved (encrypted) IRC passwords. The key that decrypts them is in database/secret.key, so keep both private.
